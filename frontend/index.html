Help Center

Getting Started

  • Create an account — Click "Sign In" → "Register" and fill in your details.
  • Browse venues — Use the search bar to filter by city, date, guests, and budget.
  • Book a venue — Click any venue card → pick a time slot → complete payment via Razorpay.
  • Get confirmation — A booking receipt is emailed to your registered address instantly.

Managing Your Bookings

  • View all bookings under Dashboard → My Bookings.
  • Download receipts from the booking details page.
  • Cancel bookings more than 48 hours in advance for a full refund.
  • Leave reviews for venues you've visited under Dashboard → My Reviews.

Payments & Security

  • All payments are processed by Razorpay (PCI-DSS Level 1 certified).
  • Accepted: UPI, credit/debit cards, net banking, wallets.
  • EVNTLY never stores your card or bank details.

For Venue Owners

  • Register using the "List Your Space" form on the homepage.
  • Upload proof documents (venue agreement / utility bills / photos).
  • After admin approval (24–48 hrs), complete payment for your chosen plan.
  • Manage bookings, slots, and revenue from your Dashboard.

Refund Policy Effective Jan 2025

Cancellation by Customer

  • More than 48 hours before the event: 100% refund to original payment method within 5–7 business days.
  • 24–48 hours before the event: 50% refund.
  • Less than 24 hours before the event: No refund.
  • All cancellation requests must be submitted via Dashboard → My Bookings.

Cancellation by Venue Owner

  • If a venue owner cancels a confirmed booking, customers receive a 100% refund within 3–5 business days.
  • EVNTLY will also help rebook an alternative venue at no extra charge.
  • Repeated cancellations by venue owners may result in account suspension.

Subscription Plans (Venue Owners)

  • Subscription fees are non-refundable once the plan is activated.
  • If an owner's application is rejected by EVNTLY admin, any plan payment made is fully refunded.

Disputes & Escalations

  • If you believe a refund has not been processed correctly, email hello@evntly.in with your booking ID.
  • EVNTLY will investigate and respond within 3 business days.

How Refunds Are Processed

Refunds are returned to the original payment method (UPI, card, net banking, or wallet) via Razorpay. Processing time: 5–7 business days depending on your bank.
